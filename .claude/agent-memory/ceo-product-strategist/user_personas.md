## Persona 1: The News Junkie Predictor
- Consumes news daily, wants to express opinions and be validated
- JTBD: "I want to be right about what happens next and have proof of it"
- Engagement driver: leaderboard, accuracy score, badges
- Entry point: mobile news feed, polls tab

## Persona 2: The Market Host / Community Builder
- Wants to run prediction games for their friend group or community
- JTBD: "I want to host fun prediction contests and earn rewards for doing it well"
- Engagement driver: host trust score, commission/bond rewards, hosting limits as a prestige ladder
- Entry point: groups feature, create market flow
- Friction point: 14-day account age + 2 valid finalized markets before trusted-host eligibility — high barrier for new users

## Persona 3: The Casual Participant
- Sees a market in their feed, makes a quick YES/NO call
- JTBD: "I want a quick way to engage with a story I just read"
- Engagement driver: insight cards, polls, crowd context
- Risk: drops off if onboarding friction is too high or if market resolution takes too long

## Persona 4: The Group Member (Private Markets)
- Participates in private friend/family/coworker prediction leagues
- JTBD: "I want a prediction game my group can play together around shared events"
- Engagement driver: group leaderboard, private market results
- Key insight: this persona has the highest retention potential because of social accountability
