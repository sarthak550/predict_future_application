## Manifold Features Confirmed Observed (2026-05-07 scan)

### Market Types
- Binary YES/NO (we have)
- Multiple Choice / Multi-option (we have — shipped S24-T10)
- Numeric range (we have)
- **Polls (non-betting, opinion-gather only — we do NOT have a clean standalone poll type separate from markets)**
- **Bounty/Free Response markets — we do NOT have**

### Special Mechanics
- **Limit orders** — set a bet at a specific probability rather than current price
- **Loans** — borrow against open positions to free up capital (daily free loans + margin loans for members)
- **Liquidity subsidies** — anyone can add mana to a market's liquidity pool
- **Tips** — send mana to good comments
- Shares/payouts locking at bet time (we have equivalent)

### Social
- Comments on markets (we may have, unconfirmed at mobile level)
- Follow traders (shipped S24-T2/T3)
- User profiles (we have)
- **Boosts** — pay mana to promote a market to more users in feed
- **Tips on comments** — micro-reward good analysis

### Discovery
- **Topic/news cluster browsing** — markets organized under live news topics (OpenAI, TikTok, etc.)
- Category filters (Politics, Tech, Sports, Culture, Business, Fun)
- Search across markets + users + topics + posts (we have some search)
- **Predictle** — daily word-game style mini-game tied to prediction markets
- **Prize Drawing** — separate sweepstakes mechanic (skip: real-money, India-irrelevant)

### Gamification
- Quests: daily prediction streaks, share markets, weekly creation, phone verify (we have quests S24-T4/T5)
- Leaderboards: profit, loss, volume, creators, referrals (we have leaderboard)
- Monthly leagues (we shipped S24-T7/T8/T9)
- Referrals (we shipped S24-T6)
- **Phone verification bonus** — extra points for verifying phone number (we may not have)
- Calibration scorecard (we shipped S24-T1)

### Platform / Other
- Dark/light mode toggle (unknown if we have)
- Web-native (desktop Manifold is primary surface — we are mobile-first, this is our advantage)
- Manifold has no India-specific content or analyst credibility layer (our moat)

## Key Gaps for Sprint 25 Consideration
Priority gaps: Limit Orders, Bounty Markets, Comment Tips, Market Boosts (feed promotion), Predictle-style mini-game, Liquid subsidies, Loans system, Topic cluster browsing.

**Why:** Limit orders and loans are complex financial mechanics that may not fit our India-first play-money positioning well. Bounty markets (crowd-sourced research questions) are a genuine gap. Topic cluster browsing is partially solved by our news feed but not organized as well. Mini-game (Predictle equivalent) would be a high-retention viral feature.
