## POSITIONING LOCK
Brand name: "India's Analyst Scorecard"
Tagline: "Track who called it right."
NEVER use "prediction market" in any public copy. The framing is: news-native analyst call-rating platform with a live public accuracy leaderboard.

## Finalized Copy Slots

**Tagline (under 8 words):** Track who called it right.

**Elevator pitch (under 25 words):** Predict Future scores every analyst call on Indian markets — so you always know which experts are worth listening to.

**App Store short description (67 chars):** Rate analyst calls. Build your track record. See who's actually right.

**Onboarding Screen 1:** Headline: "Analysts make calls. We keep score." Subtext: Every day HDFC, ICICI, and Morgan Stanley tell you where markets are headed. Now find out who's actually right.

**Onboarding Screen 2:** Headline: "Rate the call. Make your own." Subtext: Bullish or Bearish — tap once to react to a live analyst opinion, then back yourself with your own prediction.

**Onboarding Screen 3:** Headline: "Your accuracy is your reputation." Subtext: Every call you make is tracked. Build a public track record that proves your instincts — and outrank the analysts.

**Twitter/X bio (128 chars):** India's analyst scorecard. Rate every market call. Track who got it right. Build your own track record. No money, just proof.

**WhatsApp share line:** "Check this out — it's a live scorecard tracking which Indian market analysts are actually right. You can rate their calls and build your own track record. Better than arguing on fintwit."

**Press pitch (for ET/Mint):** "We built a public accuracy leaderboard for Indian sell-side analysts — the first time anyone has scored HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct, and Morgan Stanley India on whether their market calls actually came true. Retail investors are already rating calls in real time. Happy to share the first leaderboard data with you."

## Landing Page Press Strategy
The leaderboard section is the press hook. Publish live accuracy numbers for named Indian research firms without requiring login. Add methodology footer: "Accuracy = calls marked HIT / total resolved calls, minimum 5 calls to qualify." This removes the journalist's primary objection and makes the page self-contained as a quotable source.

**How to apply:** All future copy, onboarding, press outreach, and App Store updates must use this framing. Do not revert to "prediction market" language. The analyst credibility angle is the media hook — lead with it in all external communications.
