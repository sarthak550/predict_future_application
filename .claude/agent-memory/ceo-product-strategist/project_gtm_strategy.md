## POSITIONING (LOCKED 2026-05-06)
Brand positioning: "India's Analyst Scorecard"
Tagline: "Track who called it right."
The product is NO LONGER described as a "prediction market" in any public-facing copy, press, or onboarding. It is a news-native analyst call-rating platform. Users rate real sell-side analyst calls (HDFC Securities, ICICI Direct, Morgan Stanley India, etc.) and a live public credibility leaderboard tracks who was actually right.

This reframe removes regulatory ambiguity (no "market", no "bet"), increases press hook value (journalists love "which analysts were right"), and gives Indian retail investors a familiar, non-intimidating entry point.

## Product Thesis (locked)
Predict Future is a news-native analyst call-rating platform for Indian retail investors and finance-engaged consumers. Users rate breaking analyst calls as BULLISH/BEARISH/NEUTRAL, compete with analysts by making their own calls, and a live credibility leaderboard tracks which analysts (and users) have the best track record — in a feed designed like TikTok, not Bloomberg.

The core bet: most Indians who follow markets want to express opinions and test their instincts but have nowhere to do it that isn't either (a) real-money regulated, (b) boring/desktop-only, or (c) divorced from actual news.

## Target User (locked v1)
Primary: Indian finance-engaged urban consumer, 22–38, smartphone-native, follows markets casually (Zerodha/Groww user, ET Markets reader, CNBC TV18 viewer). Not a serious trader. Wants to be right, share opinions, and have proof.

Secondary: Prediction market enthusiasts who want a no-cash-risk sandbox with real news context.

## Differentiation vs Competitors (locked)
- vs Polymarket/Kalshi: No real money = no SEBI/PMLA compliance wall. Lower barrier. Pure opinion product.
- vs Manifold Markets: India-first content and analyst credibility layer. Manifold is US/global, general-purpose, requires cognitive effort to create markets.
- vs X/Twitter polls: Persistent, scored, historical. A tweet poll disappears in 24h with no resolution or accountability.
- vs Ground News: We don't compare sources — we score the experts making the calls.
- vs Robinhood Social/StockTwits: No trading, no SEBI exposure. Lighter regulatory footprint. The expert credibility scoring is a genuine moat StockTwits has never built.

## GTM Wedge (locked)
First 1,000 users: Finance Twitter (now X) and WhatsApp finance groups in India. Seed with seeded expert opinions from known voices (HDFC Securities, ICICI, etc.) so there is immediate content to rate. Use Poll A (Do you agree with this analyst?) as the frictionless first-action. No account required to view; account required to vote.

Scale path: viral via P&L card sharing, expert credibility leaderboard as media hook (journalists love "which analysts were right"), WhatsApp group invite links for group prediction leagues.

## Key Legal Flag
SEBI Research Analyst regulations need outside-counsel review before public leaderboard launch. This is a known blocker for any paid or public-facing credibility product.

**How to apply:** All sprint prioritization and GTM decisions should be filtered through this thesis. Finance-first, India-first, mobile-first. The analyst call-rating mechanic is the wedge. Everything else is retention scaffolding.
