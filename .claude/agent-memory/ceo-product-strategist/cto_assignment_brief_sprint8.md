Sprint 8 closes the most embarrassing quality gaps and builds the social layer.

## Strategic rationale

Sprint 7 completed the engagement loop (streaks, inline voting, sticky betting, sharing). Sprint 8 must now close the trust and quality gaps that would cause any new user acquired via those share links to churn immediately, and unlock the social competition layer.

## Tickets issued (2026-05-01)

| ID | Priority | Title |
|---|---|---|
| S8-T1 | critical | Groups tab: replace placeholder with functional group browse and join flow |
| S8-T2 | critical | Resolution payoff: win/loss modal with points delta when a market resolves |
| S8-T3 | critical | Notifications: make each notification tappable and route to the relevant screen |
| S8-T4 | high | Leaderboard tab: restore as a full-screen dedicated tab visible in the nav |
| S8-T5 | high | Public user profiles: tap a username anywhere to view their stats |
| S8-T6 | high | Create market: post-submission success screen and draft recovery UX |
| S8-T7 | medium | Feed: skeleton loading cards on initial load instead of a blank spinner |
| S8-T8 | medium | Market detail: resolution rationale and resolver identity in the resolved view |

## Why these eight

- S8-T1: A visible nav tab that renders placeholder text is a trust-killer for any new user acquired via Sprint 7 share links.
- S8-T2: The resolution payoff is the emotional peak of prediction markets. Currently it's a status chip. Must feel like a win.
- S8-T3: Notifications exist but tapping them does nothing. Critical for market-outcome re-engagement loops.
- S8-T4: The leaderboard screen is fully built but hidden. Social competition is the primary K-factor driver.
- S8-T5: Can't tap usernames to see profiles. Kills social layer before it starts.
- S8-T6: Create flow has no success confirmation. Builders have no feedback that their market was submitted.
- S8-T7: Blank spinner on Feed first load is a mobile UX anti-pattern. Skeleton cards look polished.
- S8-T8: Unexaplained resolution outcomes erode host trust. Rationale display is a trust signal.

**Why:** Sprints 1-7 built the product. Sprint 8 makes it feel finished enough to show to users without embarrassment.
**How to apply:** Every ticket in this sprint is about closing gaps that would cause a new user to lose trust or churn. Prioritise accordingly.
