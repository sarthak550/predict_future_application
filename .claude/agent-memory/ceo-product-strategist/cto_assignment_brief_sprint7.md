# CTO Assignment Brief — Predict Future, Sprint 7
**Issued:** 2026-05-01
**Sprint:** 7
**From:** CEO
**To:** CTO / Lead Developer
**Prior sprint status:** Sprints 1-6 (16 tickets) confirmed delivered. App is functional end-to-end with auth, market creation, betting, comments, notifications, leaderboard, profile, and web parity.

---

## Context

The foundation is solid. Users can register, predict, comment, and get notified. The question shifts entirely to: **why do they come back tomorrow?**

This sprint is about daily retention mechanics and viral loops. Every ticket in Sprint 7 answers one of three questions:
1. How do we make the first 60 seconds of a new user's experience make sense? (Onboarding)
2. How do we give users a reason to open the app every day? (Streak, P&L, inline voting)
3. How do we get users to invite other users? (Share deep links, Sports funnel)

---

## Tickets

- **S7-T1** (Critical): Feed inline poll voting — vote without leaving the feed
- **S7-T2** (Critical): Market detail sticky betting panel — CTA always visible
- **S7-T3** (Critical): First-run tooltip walkthrough — 4-step onboarding overlay
- **S7-T4** (High): Streak daily engagement mechanic — streak reminder modal, fire badge in feed header
- **S7-T5** (High): Sports tab match-to-market funnel — tap a match to see linked markets and create a prediction
- **S7-T6** (High): Profile P&L summary card — net points won/lost, API addition required
- **S7-T7** (High): Deep-link share sheet — URL in every share, share from list cards, share after poll vote
- **S7-T8** (High): Markets sort controls + trending shelf — sort by new/trending/closing/volume, top 3 trending row

---

## CEO Priorities for This Sprint

1. S7-T3 (Onboarding) — without this, acquisition spend is wasted. Every new user is confused.
2. S7-T1 (Inline poll voting) — the single highest-leverage engagement improvement. Polls are already being generated; the friction of navigating away is killing conversion.
3. S7-T2 (Sticky bet panel) — the market detail page buries the primary CTA. Fix this before we drive traffic to market pages.
